Lip Sync

Players

One name per line. Each battle draws two people who haven't gone yet this round, and you pick the winner. If a round has an odd number of people, its last battle is a 3-way. Winners move on until there's a champion.

Secret song picks

Give someone a song and it plays the first time they battle. It looks like a normal random pick on the Show screen.

Find the videos

Paste a YouTube Data API key and hit Find. It looks for the “Topic” audio upload of each song, checks the length against Spotify, and flags anything that needs a look. The key is saved only in this browser.
